KING BOO REMATCH

The fight that follows the Casino Delfino roulette fight. King Boo drops the puzzle act and fights for real.


WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT FROM EVERY OTHER FIGHT IN THE PORT

- No FLUDD. Mario arrives without it. The hose is not the answer to anything here.
- Reaction, not thinking. Every other King Boo fight in Mario is a puzzle. This one is a dodge test built on Sunshine's tight movement.
- Three hits, and Mario's only weapon is a Boo he throws back at him.
- Fast and upbeat.


INTRO

1. The roulette fight ends. The casino floor takes a cracked glass effect, then shatters.
2. Mario falls through the floor into a load trigger, which loads the new scene.
3. The screen fades to black and two lines appear:
- "You call that a fight fit for a king?"
- "I'll show you a real fight..."
4. Fade in on Mario falling into the arena. He has no FLUDD.
5. King Boo spawns in and gives a long chuckle.
6. The fight starts.


ARENA

KING BOO

Look. A slight glow on him throughout.

Movement. He is a ghost, so he is quick and light. He teleports between spots around the arena, or crosses to one fast, the way Thunderblight Ganon moves in Breath of the Wild. He does not walk anywhere.

Faces. Three of them, one per hit point, each angrier than the last.


PACE AND ATTACK SELECTION

Hectic by design. He picks his next attack close to at random rather than running a fixed rotation, and the gap between attacks is short. Each hit he takes shortens that gap further, so the last third of the fight is the fastest. The player should rarely be standing still.


KING BOO'S ATTACKS

1. LICK
No crown colour. He teleports directly in front of Mario and licks, with half a second of wind-up before it lands. The wind-up is the whole tell and a jump clears it.

2. BOOLOSSUS SMASH
Crown glows white. A Boolossus falls from above Mario and tries to slam down on him. On landing it splits into 15 Boos that spread out as a shockwave, then vanish after one second.

3. GHOST LIGHTNING
Crown glows red. Two walls of lightning close in on Mario from either side and try to squash him. Three times in a row, fast.

4. GHOST BREATH
Crown glows frosty blue. He blows cold breath in a sweep across the arena, left to right or right to left. A hit freezes Mario for one second.

5. FIREBALLS
Crown glows red. Fireballs shoot off in random directions and bounce off the arena walls. They shrink as they travel and fade out.

6. GHOST WIND
Crown glows pink, for now. Tornados come at Mario and fling him around the arena.


WHEN AN ATTACK CONNECTS

No new damage model. Each attack hands Mario the reaction Sunshine already has for that element, through the existing Mario.SmsDamageProfile and SmsDamageExec.

- Lick and Boolossus Smash: the plain hit, knocked down and back. The knockback comes off the profile's downType and minSpeed fields.
- Fireballs: the burn. Set fireDown on the profile, which routes into SmsFireDownEnter and the ms_firedmg_a cloud.
- Ghost Lightning: the shock. SmsElectricDamageEnter, which already locks Mario for 120 ticks and runs the ma_shock clip with its sparks and closing puff.
- Ghost Wind: knocked back and flung. Nothing to reuse here. The Chucksters are not in the port, so there is no thrown-by-a-Pianta state to borrow, and the fling is new work either way.
- Ghost Breath: the one reaction Sunshine does not have. There is no ice or freeze anywhere in the game, so the one second freeze is a new state.


HOW MARIO DEALS DAMAGE: BOO BOMBS

The black Boos from Mario Galaxy. This is the only way to hurt King Boo.

- Black foggy spots appear at random through the fight and a Boo bomb comes out of each one. Mario has to notice them and get to them.
- Grounded spin next to one and Mario grabs it. It then spins above his head, the way the Galaxy bomb does.
- While carrying: Mario can run and single jump, nothing else. A spin jump makes the Boo spin faster.
- Diving drops it. The Boo is released and goes back to drifting toward Mario.
- Land the spinning Boo on King Boo and he takes a hit. He flips around in a hurt animation.
- Every Boo bomb on the field clears when he takes a hit.

Supply. The spawn rate, the cap on how many are out at once and whether one expires on its own are all knobs, tuned in playtest. The target to tune against: often enough that the fight is not pure dodging with no way to answer, rare enough that it is not easy.
